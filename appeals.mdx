---
title: "Appeals and Revocations"
---

Appeals seek relief from a valid, appealable punishment based on explanation and learning. Open an IA/staff appeal ticket. Warnings and demotions are not appealable.

Revocation removes a false punishment; document why it was incorrect. Only review punishments issued by ranks below yours; escalate others.

<Warning>Source commands `/appeal (case id) (ticket id)` and `/revoke (case id) (reason)` are not verified. Appeal deadline, channel, and detailed PTS procedure remain pending. BOD+ are PTS-exempt.</Warning>
