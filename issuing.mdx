---
title: "Issuing Punishments"
---

Gather facts and evidence; consider a conversation or warning for minor first offenses. Select action and appealability. Obtain SHR+ approval, issue through the configured system, and retain proof, approval reference, and reasoning.

Request original recordings or corroboration where screenshots/DMs may be edited.

HR may depart from the guide with a documented reason without separate deviation approval. The punishment itself still requires SHR+ approval.

<Warning>The source command `/infract (user) (punishment) (appealable) (reason) (proof)` is not verified for ISRP.</Warning>
