---
title: "Leadership Responsibilities"
---

<Accordion title="Internal Affairs">

Handles IA cases and staff discipline with proof and prior SHR+ approval.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Management">

Handles Management tickets and day-to-day operations; HR disciplinary rules apply.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Supervisory">

Supervises staff, approves punishments and 50/50, and processes transfers. Handles assigned leadership/BOD tickets without acquiring all BOD permissions.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Board of Directors">

Improves and runs operations when Ownership is absent. Hosts STS. BOD+ exempt from ticket PTS.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Ownership">

Runs the community, oversees teams, and directs approved growth and partnerships.

</Accordion>


### Ownership
Owner → Co Owner → Assistant Owner

### Board of Directors
Director → Deputy Director → Assistant Director

### Supervisory Team
Senior Supervisor → Head Supervisor → Supervisor → Trial Supervisor

### Management Team
Senior Management → Head Management → Management → Trial Management

### Internal Affairs Team
Senior Internal Affairs → Head Internal Affairs → Internal Affairs → Trial Internal Affairs

### Administration Team
Senior Administration → Head Administration → Administration → Trial Administration

### Moderation Team
Senior Moderator → Head Moderator → Moderator → Trial Moderator

HR = Internal Affairs and Management. SHR = Supervisory. TA = Trial Administration. TMGMT = Trial Management. HMGMT = Head Management. A + includes all higher ranks. Senior is above Head in the supplied order.
