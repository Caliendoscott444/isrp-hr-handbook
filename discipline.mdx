---
title: "Punishments and Escalation"
---

Every staff punishment requires proof and prior SHR+ approval. HR may issue the approved action.

- Warning: first minor offense; not appealable. Repetition leads to an infraction.
- Infraction: minor misconduct; normally appealable.
- Three active, upheld infractions become a strike. If the third is appealable, wait until its appeal resolves or the established deadline passes.
- Strike: serious misconduct; normally appealable, unless a justified non-appealable action is recorded.
- Four strikes result in termination.
- Demotion: unfit for current rank; rare and not appealable.
- Termination: serious misconduct or unfitness for staff; SHR+ approval is required.
- Infractions and strikes expire after five weeks.
- Investigation may temporarily remove permissions.

<Warning>The appeal deadline and automation are not configured. Do not assume a bot handles escalation or expiry.</Warning>
